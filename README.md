# Learning in Practice

Status: v0.1 draft · Collection framework · No field reports published

Learning in Practice records how Trivian Institute educational resources are tried, questioned, and improved in real settings. It is a home for bounded teaching accounts and periodic synthesis across the Educational Commons. A report may describe a useful moment, difficulty, or failure; none is proof that a program works.

**Start with [How to contribute a field note](START_HERE.md).** If you are planning a session, begin in [Educator Commons](https://github.com/TrivianInstitute/educator-commons). If you have no suitable permission to share an account, keep a private reflection and contribute a general suggestion instead.

## Scope and ethics

Do not upload raw student work, identifiable quotes, names, contact details, screenshots, private prompts, health or disability details, grades, classroom rosters, or small-group combinations that could identify someone. This includes issues, pull requests, and commit history: removing a file later does not reliably erase copies. We do not solicit evidence from minors through GitHub. For youth settings, an authorized adult should follow institutional and local processes before considering any non-identifying, aggregated report. Do not represent educational participation as research consent.

A facilitator's observation is valuable and limited. Describe what happened, explain the setting and access choices, consider another explanation, and name what you would change next. A positive account is not effectiveness evidence; a critical one is not a failure to support the mission.
