# Contributing to Learning in Practice

Status: v0.1 draft · Contact: learn@trivianinstitute.org

Start with [How to contribute](START_HERE.md) and the [publication boundaries](publication/privacy-and-consent.md). This is a public repository; do not paste real field accounts into issues, PRs, or comments for a safety check. Ask a general, non-identifying question or contact the Institute privately before sharing any detail.

## Ways to contribute

- Propose a correction or improvement to a source resource, with no learner narrative.
- Draft an eligible, de-identified field note using the [template](templates/field-note.md), after securing authority to publish from the setting.
- Improve the submission method or propose a synthesis grounded in reviewed public notes.
- Identify an accessibility barrier without describing the person who encountered it.

For any field note, distinguish direct observation, interpretation, another explanation, limits, and a concrete next change. Do not convert a workshop account into proof of effectiveness. For youth settings, only an authorized adult may consider an aggregated account after following relevant institutional, caregiver, and safeguarding processes. We do not solicit submissions from minors through GitHub.

## Rights and review

Submit only text you are authorized to license under [CC BY-SA 4.0](LICENSE). Contributors retain copyright; no assignment is requested. Do not reproduce learner work, identifiable quotations, images, logs, private curricula, or third-party material without an appropriate rights and publication review. A maintainer may narrow, return, or decline a contribution for privacy, rights, evidence, or scope reasons. Never post sensitive content in a PR even temporarily.

If AI substantially assisted your drafting or analysis, disclose the provider/system if known, approximate date, tasks, material human changes, and checks. Do not disclose private prompts or learner data to prove it. The contributor remains responsible for accuracy, permissions, accessibility, and claims.

See [community expectations](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). For a private publication question, contact learn@trivianinstitute.org.
