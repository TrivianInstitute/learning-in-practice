# Submission and editorial review

## Eligible contributions

- A field note following [the template](templates/field-note.md) from a setting where public reporting and the proposed license are permitted, with no identifying or sensitive material.
- A correction to an existing public note, with a clear revision history and link.
- A resource improvement suggestion that needs no field narrative.
- A synthesis grounded in listed public notes, with limitations visible.

Do not post a real report in a GitHub issue to ask whether it is safe. A public issue is already publication.

## Before opening a PR

- Confirm authorization from your institution or setting if required. Confirm your right to share your own contribution under this repository's CC BY-SA 4.0 terms. Never attempt to grant rights in someone else's speech, image, writing, or private data.
- Strip names, exact dates/locations, small-group details, unique incidents, recognizable quotations, screenshots, prompts, and learner work.
- State the source resource, intended activity, any adaptation, and who chose it. Use broad setting information only where it helps interpretation and does not identify people.
- Separate direct observation from interpretation; provide at least one plausible alternative explanation.
- State what did not work or remains unknown, including access barriers.
- Propose one concrete change and describe how its effect might be noticed later.
- Disclose substantive AI assistance: provider/system if known, approximate date, task, changes made by you, and verification. Never paste private prompts or learner data.

## Maintainer review

A maintainer checks scope, rights, privacy, potential indirect identification, claim level, accessibility, and relevance. They may request aggregation, omit detail, seek a private follow-up, or decline. A report can be accepted with unresolved questions if those questions remain visible. Revisions are recorded in the PR; sensitive content should never be posted there, even temporarily.

## Publication state

Mark each accepted note “single setting, self-reported,” with date of publication, a broad context, and a link to the resource version used. If a correction changes the interpretation, add a dated correction notice. A collection of such notes is still not a controlled study, outcome measurement, or validation of the underlying framework.

Contact learn@trivianinstitute.org for a nonpublic question before sharing details.
