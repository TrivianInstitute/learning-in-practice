# Privacy and publication boundaries

This is a public GitHub repository. Issues, PRs, comments, and commits can be copied or indexed. Do not share sensitive details here for a maintainer to clean up later.

## Keep out of public submissions

- Names, aliases that are recognizable locally, faces, voices, email addresses, handles, and school or workplace rosters.
- Direct learner quotations, writing, screenshots, prompt logs, grades, attendance, and individual feedback.
- Health, disability, trauma, immigration, family, relationship, or disciplinary details.
- Exact dates and places or unusual combinations of age, setting, role, group size, and incident that could reveal a person.
- Any account of a minor's personal AI interaction or a safeguarding concern.

Removing names alone may not prevent re-identification. If a detail is needed to explain a teaching issue but could identify someone, describe the issue at a higher level or choose not to publish. Never fabricate consent or treat a blanket class participation form as permission to license learner contributions.

## Decision path

1. **Authority:** Are you permitted to share a teaching account from this setting? If unsure, stop before a public draft.
2. **Necessity:** Can the lesson be stated without a learner narrative? Prefer a general improvement suggestion.
3. **Minimization:** Keep only the details needed to understand the activity and the observed pattern.
4. **Review:** Ask an appropriate institutional reviewer when required. For youth, follow existing safeguarding and caregiver/student participation processes.
5. **Publication:** Submit only an already reviewed, non-identifying account and a statement that you are authorized to license your own contribution. This does not ask you to publish proof of anyone's consent.

An individual's permission may still be insufficient when other people or an institution are implicated. Do not put copies of consent forms, signatures, or private correspondence in this repository. Publication can be declined even after permission.

This guide is an editorial boundary, not legal advice or a substitute for local rules. For a private question, write learn@trivianinstitute.org without attaching raw learner records.
